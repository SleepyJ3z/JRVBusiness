Comprehensive Office Solutions

We don't just supply furniture; we engineer workspaces. From ground-up minor renovations to the meticulous installation of window and floor coverings, our in-house team handles every detail.

Office System Setup & Installation

Transitioning to a new office or upgrading your current layout requires precision. We handle the complete assembly and spatial arrangement of all office systems. This includes modular desking, executive suites, ergonomic seating, and the secure installation of metal or wood cabinets.

  • ✅ Partitions: Custom sizing and acoustic optimization.
  • ✅ Desking: Cable management and ergonomic alignment.
  • ✅ Signage: Precision mounting of custom acrylic signs.

Floor & Window Coverings

The right textures define the atmosphere of a room. We supply and install premium commercial-grade coverings designed for high traffic and aesthetic longevity.

  • ✅ Flooring: Carpet (tile/roll), Vinyl Planks, SPC, and Laminated options.
  • ✅ Windows: Combi shades and mini horizontal blinds for light control.
  • ✅ Walls: Professional wallcoverings for both residential and commercial spaces.

Renovations, Reupholstery & Hauling

We believe in sustainability and efficiency. Before you replace everything, let our experts assess your current assets. We can breathe new life into old furniture or efficiently clear out spaces for new beginnings.

  • ✅ Minor Renovations: Structural adjustments to optimize your floor plan.
  • ✅ Reupholstery: Premium fabric and leather restoration for worn seating.
  • ✅ Hauling: Safe, rapid removal of outdated office equipment and debris.
